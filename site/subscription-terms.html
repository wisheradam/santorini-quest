Subscription Terms

Last updated: October 11, 2026

If you actively select the optional subscription checkbox, Santorini.quest may email travel news, special offers, promotions, new travel experiences, and partner offers. Subscription is never required to receive your requested Travel Adventure.

Consent to partner offers does not authorize us to transfer your personal information to a partner. Message frequency may vary. You can unsubscribe at any time through the link in a promotional email, or contact travel@santorini.quest.

Unsubscribing affects promotional messages only; it does not prevent a transactional email you specifically request.
